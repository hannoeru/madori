# Research: zh-TW (Traditional Chinese) Japan/Tokyo property portals used by Taiwanese buyers

## Summary
Two portal types serve zh-TW buyers of Tokyo property: (1) Taiwan-bred Japan brokerages with native Traditional-Chinese sites — 信義日本 / 信義房屋日本置產 (Sinyi), 樂房東京不動產 (thefun.com.tw), 國際東京不動產 (jhouse.com.tw), JPHome — and (2) Japan-side portals with zh-TW localization — Japan-Property, Real Estate Japan, 三井不動產Realty, 住友不動產Step. Three portals named in the brief do **not** list Japan property: 樂屋網 (rakuya.com.tw) and 591 are Taiwan-domestic platforms, and FUNWOO is US-only. The best zh-TW buyer model combines Sinyi's Taiwan conventions (坪 + 万円, Japanese address kept raw) with the localized multilingual model (區/站, m²/平方公尺, 日幣).

## Findings

### 1. Portal landscape (corrected against primary sources)

1. **信義日本 (Sinyi Japan) / 信義房屋日本置產** — the dominant Taiwan-operated Japan brokerage. In Japan 15 years, ~6,000 units sold, ~2,000 units under lease management, 20% repurchase rate. Self-serve zh-TW search at `sinyi.com.tw/buy/jp` and `sinyijapan.com/tw`. [Sinyi buy/jp](https://www.sinyi.com.tw/buy/jp) · [Sinyi Japan](https://www.sinyijapan.com/tw)

2. **Japan-Property (japan-property.jp/zh-TW)** — multilingual portal (English / 简体中文 / 繁體中文 / ไทย / 日本語) with full zh-TW search and detail pages; agents (e.g. 東急Livable) post listings directly. [Tokyo sale list](https://www.japan-property.jp/zh-TW/property-for-sale/Tokyo) · [Detail example](https://www.japan-property.jp/zh-TW/apartment-property-for-sale-in-tokyo-S0029222)

3. **Real Estate Japan (realestate.co.jp/zh_TW)** — zh-TW localized sale+rent portal with full zh-TW field rendering. A near-duplicate UI exists at japan-homes.com/zh-TW (same listing DB; machine-translation leaks Korean "관리비"). [Real Estate Japan zh_TW](https://realestate.co.jp/zh_TW) · [Japan Homes zh-TW](https://www.japan-homes.com/zh-TW/listings/)

4. **三井不動產Realty (global.mf-realty.jp/tc)** — Japanese major with an auto-translated Traditional-Chinese self-serve search; page carries "本網頁為自動翻譯，內容並非100%準確實" disclaimer. [Mitsui tc search](https://global.mf-realty.jp/tc/searchResidentialPropertyResults/w-13102/)

5. **樂房東京不動產 (thefun.com.tw)** — Taiwan-side brokerage (Kyoto/Tokyo/Kobe); raw Japanese addresses, 万円 prices, yield%. [樂房](https://thefun.com.tw/)

6. **國際東京不動產 (jhouse.com.tw)** — Taiwan-side Tokyo brokerage; converts to 台幣 + 坪, shows 投報率 (yield %). [jhouse](https://www.jhouse.com.tw/)

7. **JPHome (jp-home.com)** — zh-TW Tokyo brokerage with purpose/region filters; mixes 簡/繁 (関東, 東京23区). [JPHome search](https://www.jp-home.com/p_search.asp?clearall=0&p_intro=1)

8. **Consultation-led Taiwan-facing entries** (limited self-serve search): 東急Livable台灣 [livable.co.jp/biz/taiwan](https://www.livable.co.jp/biz/taiwan/), 住友不動產Step [global.sumitomo-res.com/tw](https://global.sumitomo-res.com/tw/), 精靈不動產 [genieverse.co.jp](https://genieverse.co.jp/), GTN/BEST-ESTATE purchase support [best-estate.jp/tw](https://www.best-estate.jp/tw/purchase/).

9. **神居秒算 (zh.shenjumiaosuan.com)** — GA Technologies "大中華圈" platform (serves China/Taiwan/HK). Simplified-Chinese-leaning: 萬日元, walk distance in 米 (meters), mixed 簡/繁. [神居秒算](https://zh.shenjumiaosuan.com/)

10. **Not Japan portals (misidentified in the brief):**
    - **樂屋網 (rakuya.com.tw)** — Taiwan-domestic portal (台北市/新北市/桃園…), aggregates 信義/住商/中信/東森/21世紀/太平洋/台灣房屋/永慶 **Taiwan** listings. No Japan inventory. [rakuya.com.tw](https://www.rakuya.com.tw/) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rakuya.mobile)
    - **591 (591.com.tw)** — Taiwan-domestic platform. Search hits for "東京" are a Taichung condo community named "東京市" (sub-buildings 澀谷區/銀座), not Japan listings. [591](https://www.591.com.tw/)
    - **FUNWOO (funwoo.com.tw)** — US-only overseas brokerage (紐約/西雅圖/加州/波士頓/鳳凰城). No Japan inventory. [FUNWOO](https://www.funwoo.com.tw/) · [FUNWOO 美國建案](https://funwoo.com.tw/building)

### 2. Buyer-facing fields for a single sell listing

Core fields common to all portals: price, area (專有面積), layout (LDK), address, nearest station + walk time (交通), property type (公寓/獨棟住宅/整棟/土地), age (完工/竣工日月), floor / total floors, land rights (土地權利), and photos + floor plan.

Portal-specific field lists worth mirroring:

- **Sinyi detail page** (new-build): 物件編號, 住址, 類型, 交通, 基地面積, 竣工年月, 格局, 建物結構 (RC), 賣主, 樓高, 專有面積, 總戶數, 施工, 土地權利, 官網, 最新更新日, 建案位置 (map). Card shows: name, address, 坪, 屋齡, 格局, 總戶數, 車站/歩分, 新築案場, price range (万円). [Sinyi detail JP793895](https://www.sinyi.com.tw/buy/jp/project/JP793895)

- **Japan-Property detail page** (resale): price (日幣), 房地產種類, 完工日期, 每月管理費 (incl. 修繕積立金), 樓層 (11/34), 土地權利, 交通 (line + station + 步行分鐘), 格局 as 房/廳/衛, 陽台面積, 建築物結構, 總戶數, 管理形式, 管理方法, 現況 (空置), 交屋日期, 交易形態 (仲介), Property Code, Agency Property Code, 更新日期, agent/service-language block. [Japan-Property detail](https://www.japan-property.jp/zh-TW/apartment-property-for-sale-in-tokyo-S0029222)

- **Real Estate Japan card**: 格局, 東京都XX區, price (萬日元), m², 車站 (步行X分鐘). [REJ zh_TW](https://realestate.co.jp/zh_TW)

- **樂房 card**: price (万円), address, station (line・駅), 收益率%. [樂房](https://thefun.com.tw/)

- **國際東京不動產 card**: title, 總價 as 台幣/日元, 每坪台幣, 投報率%. [jhouse](https://www.jhouse.com.tw/)

### 4. Units

- **Area — split by portal type.** Taiwan-side brokerages use **坪** (1 坪 ≈ 3.3058 m²): Sinyi buy/jp cards show "16.77~16.93坪"; Sinyi Japan's marketing page shows ㎡ primary with 坪 in parentheses — "專有面積：37.09㎡ ~ 65.71㎡（約 11.22坪 ~ 19.88坪)". jhouse computes "每坪 X 萬台幣". Japanese/multilingual portals use **m²/㎡** labels with "平方公尺" in prose (Japan-Property detail "專有面積48.92平方公尺的2LDK"). Filter bands use ㎡. [Sinyi](https://www.sinyi.com.tw/buy/jp) · [Sinyi Japan](https://www.sinyijapan.com/tw) · [jhouse](https://www.jhouse.com.tw/) · [Japan-Property](https://www.japan-property.jp/zh-TW/apartment-property-for-sale-in-tokyo-S0029222)

- **Price — 万円 family with per-portal character choice.** 万円/萬日圓 (Sinyi, 樂房), 日幣 (Japan-Property), 日圓 (Mitsui), 日元 (Real Estate Japan), 萬日元 (神居秒算). Dual-currency appears only on Taiwan brokerages: jhouse "總價:3950萬台幣/15800萬日元". Sinyi and the Japanese portals do **not** show a TWD conversion by default. [Sources](https://www.jhouse.com.tw/) · [Japan-Property](https://www.japan-property.jp/zh-TW/apartment-property-for-sale-in-tokyo-S0029222) · [Mitsui](https://global.mf-realty.jp/tc/searchResidentialPropertyResults/w-13102/)

- **Walk distance** — zh-TW portals use 步行X分鐘 (Japan-Property, REJ); Japanese raw sites use 徒歩X分 (Sinyi); 神居秒算 uses meters (China convention). [Japan-Property](https://www.japan-property.jp/zh-TW/apartment-property-for-sale-in-tokyo-S0029222) · [Sinyi](https://www.sinyi.com.tw/buy/jp/project/JP793895)

### 5. Japanese address & station rendering for a zh-TW reader (three tiers observed)

1. **Raw Japanese (no conversion).** Sinyi (信義) keeps 区/線/駅/丁目/番 as-is: "東京都品川区西五反田４丁目646番1 他", "東急目黒線・不動前駅徒歩3分". Same on 樂房: "京都市下京区玉津島町311", "京都市営烏丸線四条駅". JPHome mixes: "関東", "東京23区". [Sinyi](https://www.sinyi.com.tw/buy/jp/project/JP793895) · [樂房](https://thefun.com.tw/) · [JPHome](https://www.jp-home.com/p_search.asp?clearall=0&p_intro=1)

2. **zh-TW localization (区→區, 駅→站/車站, 県→縣).** Real Estate Japan fully localizes: "東京都澀谷區", "東京都品川區/目黑區/豐島區", "惠比壽站(步行11分鐘)", "神奈川縣橫濱市青葉區", and for kanji-heavy stations keeps a parenthetical original "藤丘(藤が丘)站". Japan-Property localizes structure but inconsistently keeps the Japanese-only kanji "豊島區" and mixes "要町站" vs "要町車站". Mitsui tc uses "中央區", "車站 (勝鬨)". [REJ](https://realestate.co.jp/zh_TW) · [Japan-Property](https://www.japan-property.jp/zh-TW/apartment-property-for-sale-in-tokyo-S0029222) · [Mitsui](https://global.mf-realty.jp/tc/searchResidentialPropertyResults/w-13102/)

3. **Auto-translation with explicit disclaimer.** Mitsui's tc pages state "本網頁為自動翻譯" and render 中央區/車站/m² strictly via machine translation. [Mitsui](https://global.mf-realty.jp/tc/searchResidentialPropertyResults/w-13102/)

## Gaps
- Sinyi's buy/jp 價格/坪數/房數 dropdown value ranges were not expanded in the fetched text (labels confirmed, exact band values not captured) — worth a direct crawl if exact ranges matter.
- FUNWOO: official copy repeatedly states US-only, but a hidden Japan page could not be definitively ruled out; no Japan listing URL was found.
- Status fields (現況/交屋日期/交易形態) and management-fee breakdown are well-covered on Japan-Property but appear only sometimes on Taiwan-side brokerages; assume optionality in the Madori schema rather than a fixed set.

## Recommendation for Madori zh-TW buyer experience (from the evidence)
- Render addresses with zh-TW localization (区→區, 県→縣, 駅→站) but keep Japanese building names and station line names; for stations with kana/rare kanji include a parenthetical Japanese original (REJ's "藤丘(藤が丘)站" pattern).
- Show area in ㎡ primary with 坪 in parentheses (Sinyi's "37.09㎡（約 11.22坪）" pattern), and allow 坪-filtering since Taiwan buyers think in 坪.
- Price in 万円/日幣 by default; offer optional 台幣 estimate conversion (jhouse pattern) rather than a hard-coded dual price.
- Provide 格局 both as LDK (K/DK/LDK/SLDK) and zh-TW 房/廳/衛 expansion; keep 步行X分鐘 walk time.
- Filters to support at minimum: 東京 23 區 (ward), price range, 坪數/㎡ range, 格局 (rooms), property type (公寓/獨棟住宅/整棟/土地), 屋齡/finished year, and station/line.
