# Madori glossary

- **Administrator**: The one person who manages Madori Listings in the dashboard.
- **Buyer**: A zh-TW user who searches Madori for a home to buy.
- **Listing**: A new or resale condominium or detached house offered for sale in Tokyo’s 23 wards.
- **Tokyo 23 wards**: The geographic boundary for every Madori Listing in the MVP.
