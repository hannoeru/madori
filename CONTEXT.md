# Madori glossary

- **Agent**: The one real-estate agent who owns this site. The name, company, license number and LINE link are in `app/app.config.ts`.
- **Customer**: A Taiwanese visitor who looks at Listings and contacts the Agent by LINE. Customers have no account.
- **Owner**: The Agent when logged in to the management pages with the admin password.
- **Listing**: One home that the Owner manages on the site. A Listing is a Draft or a Published Listing.
- **Draft**: A Listing that only the Owner can see.
- **Published Listing**: A Listing that Customers can see. It must have its area and at least one Property photo.
- **Property photo**: One of up to twenty images of a Listing. One Property photo is the cover.
- **Floor plan**: The optional single image that shows the layout of a Listing.
- **Transit entry**: A rail line, a station and the walking time for a Listing.
- **Tokyo 23 wards**: The geographic boundary for every Listing.
